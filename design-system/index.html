Rules

  1. Clips are imagery only: no headlines, labels, callouts, numbers or keys. Text goes on in the edit or in the post.
  2. Every drawing, phone and screen is centred both ways in its card.
  3. One lime element on screen at a time: a lime part, the trace dot, or one ring.
  4. Two modes. Light: light cards on the dark ground. Dark: dark scene cards (#141416) on the same ground, drawn in light lines.
  5. Line weight follows the phone on verstack.ai: proportional to the drawing's size, outer contour heavier than the lines inside it. Appliances have softly rounded corners.
  6. App UI is real captures only. Never redraw, recolour or edit a screen.
  7. The scan gradient is rare: one faint sweep, never a cut, never on every card.
  8. New clips go in src/clips.js and must pass python3 design-system/scripts/verify.py.
